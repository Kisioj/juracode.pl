4. Cookies

The juracode.pl website does not use cookies for analytics or marketing, does not use tracking tools (such as Google Analytics or advertising pixels), and does not load resources from third-party servers such as fonts or maps. For this reason, we do not display a cookie consent banner.

Our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, may in specific situations set a technical cookie used solely to protect against bots and attacks (e.g. __cf_bm or cf_clearance). Such cookies are strictly necessary for the secure operation of the website and are not used for tracking. You can change how cookies are handled in your browser settings at any time.

5. Recipients of data

We share data only with entities that help us run our business, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. – website hosting, DNS, content delivery network (CDN), protection against attacks and forwarding of email sent to juracode.pl addresses;
  • Web3Forms – forwarding contact form messages to our mailbox;
  • Google (Gmail) – our email service;
  • providers of accounting, legal and IT services to us;
  • public authorities – only where we are legally required to disclose data.

These entities process data under contracts with us or as independent controllers, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some of our providers (in particular Cloudflare, Google and Web3Forms) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. In such cases, transfers are based on a European Commission adequacy decision (including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified entities) or on standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). You may ask us for information about the safeguards applied.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectify your data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erase your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise your rights, email kontakt@juracode.pl. We will respond without undue delay and in any event within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl) – or with the authority in your country of residence.

8. Providing data

Providing data is voluntary; however, without an email address we cannot respond to your message, and without the data necessary to conclude a contract we cannot conclude it. Providing data required to issue an invoice is a statutory requirement.

9. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, e.g. due to changes in the law or in the services we use. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top. In case of discrepancies, the Polish version prevails.
